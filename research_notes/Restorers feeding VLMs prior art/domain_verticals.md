# Domain-specific pipelines where restoration/reconstruction feeds a VLM/MLLM/LLM (documents/OCR, medical, remote sensing, driving/embodied, security/scientific), Jan 2024 to Sep 2026

Scope note. This file covers only the five verticals assigned. Generic natural-image restore-then-MLLM work, agentic restoration and generic solver+text papers are handled by another researcher (see the sibling file `inverse_solvers_text_vlm.md`); a few that surfaced here are listed briefly under "Out-of-scope items encountered" in KQ1. Venue verification method is stated per paper. "Verified" means I read the venue from the arXiv comment line, the CVF/MICCAI/NeurIPS proceedings page, a Crossref record, or the project page; "unverified" means I only saw it in a search snippet. Today's date is 2026-09-23.

Per-paper record fields used below: venue+year (how verified); ID; domain; restorer; downstream model; explicit inverse-problem (IP) formulation; posterior/uncertainty propagated downstream; answerability/identifiability assessed; key finding; threat to F1 / F2 / F3 with one-line reasons.

Formulations (from the assignment): F1 = answer identifiability via MLLM answer entropy over measurement-consistent posterior samples x ~ p(x|y), split into measurement-determined / prior-determined / undetermined; F2 = hypothesis-conditioned evidence p(y|q,a) from a text-conditioned inverse solver run per candidate answer, Bayes-factor decision; F3 = data-consistency guidance added to a unified MLLM's own generative self-restoration.

## KQ1: In each vertical, which accepted works restore/reconstruct and then feed a VLM/MLLM/LLM? Restorer, downstream model, result?

### Takeaway
Across all five verticals I found no accepted paper (Jan 2024 to Sep 2026) that runs an explicit inverse-problem solver (known operator + data consistency or posterior sampling) and then feeds the output to a VLM/MLLM/LLM. The accepted "restore-then-downstream" precedents feed detectors, segmenters or OCR (Mind the Detail, MICCAI 2025; GACR, ECCV 2026; TAIR, ICLR 2026), while the accepted VLM-side papers evaluate MLLMs directly on degraded inputs without restoration (KIE-HVQA, NeurIPS 2025; DriveBench, ICCV 2025). The only pipelines that put a physics-based reconstruction in front of a VLM-family model are arXiv-only (PASS, OmniMRI, CRT), collected in the appendix in KQ5.

#### (b) Medical imaging (reconstruction/restoration before report generation, VQA or diagnosis)

- **Mind the Detail: Uncovering Clinically Relevant Image Details in Accelerated MRI with Semantically Diverse Reconstructions (SDR)** — Jan Nikolas Morshuis et al. (Tübingen) — **MICCAI 2025** (verified: arXiv comment "MICCAI 2025"; Springer LNCS chapter 10.1007/978-3-032-04937-7_34) — arXiv 2507.00670 — Domain: accelerated knee MRI (fastMRI+). Restorer: given an initial reconstruction, SDR optimizes new reconstructions to maximize semantic distance in the feature space of an adversarially robust ViTDet encoder while enforcing **k-space data consistency** ("replacing the k-space data with the original measured data at the locations where a measurement was acquired"); N_rec = 3 reconstructions per image; ~3 s per image. Downstream: a **Faster R-CNN pathology detector** trained on fastMRI+; detections across the 3 reconstructions are merged by NMS (IoU >= 0.25) and probability averaging (score 0 for reconstructions lacking the box). IP: **Y** (undersampling mask, k-space projection). Posterior propagated: **Y** (multiple data-consistent reconstructions -> detector -> aggregated decision). Answerability: **partly** (shows existing methods, "even when considering resampling for diffusion-based reconstruction, can fail to reconstruct small and rare pathologies", i.e. false negatives). Result: higher recall (fewer false negatives) vs DDIP, CSGM and E2E-VarNet with similar or better mAP@0.25. — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.00670); [Springer](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-04937-7_34); [code](https://github.com/NikolasMorshuis/SDR). Downstream is a detector, not a VLM (**non-VLM precedent**). Threat: F1 **medium-high** (accepted precedent for "several measurement-consistent reconstructions -> downstream decision, aggregated", and for the observation that posterior resampling under-explores rare pathologies; it does not compute answer entropy or split measurement- vs prior-determined); F2 low-medium (diversity is feature-space driven, not hypothesis-conditioned; no evidence comparison); F3 none.

Answerability: proxy (task-level uncertainty). — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18026). ### Inferences
- The accepted literature is split along a line the user's formulations would cross: physics-based/inverse-problem reconstruction papers (MICCAI, ECCV) stop at detectors, segmenters or volumetrics, while VLM-robustness papers (NeurIPS, ICCV) start from an already-degraded image with no forward model. Nobody at an accepted venue has put a data-consistent posterior sampler in front of an MLLM in any of the five verticals.
- The Tübingen pair (DGM4MICCAI 2024; MICCAI 2025) is the most important non-VLM precedent: measurement-consistent sample diversity is generated deliberately, fed to a downstream decision model, and aggregated; the 2025 paper also documents that standard posterior resampling under-covers rare pathologies, which is the exact failure mode F1's "prior-determined" bucket would flag.
- In documents, the community's answer to degraded-input hallucination has been to train the MLLM to refuse (KIE-HVQA) rather than to model the measurement; in driving, to benchmark corruption (DriveBench) rather than to restore.

## KQ2 (medical): Has anyone connected physics-based reconstruction (known operator, data consistency, DPS-style sampling) to a medical VLM, VQA model or report generator, and propagated posterior samples into answers/reports?

### Takeaway
No accepted paper does this. The closest is arXiv-only: PASS (Apr 2026) wraps a physics-based unrolled MRI reconstruction with a CLIP-derived anomaly prior and evaluates reconstructions with a VLM-based anomaly detector (deterministic, no posterior), and OmniMRI (Aug 2025) is a single vision-language model that lists both "reconstruct from under-sampled MRI" and "generate a radiology report" as instructions but gives no forward operator, no data consistency and no reconstruction-then-report evaluation. No paper, accepted or not, propagates posterior samples of a reconstruction into a VQA answer or report.

### Cited Findings
- **PASS: Vision-Language Model-Guided Deep Unrolling Enables Personalized, Fast MRI** — Fangmao Ju, Yuzhu He, Zhiwen Xue, Chunfeng Lian, Jianhua Ma — arXiv 2604.06849 (Apr 8, 2026; **no venue on arXiv**) — Objective X* = argmin ||A_Phi X - Y||^2 + mu1 ||X - D_w(X)||^2 + mu2 H(NetAD(X), X) with A_Phi = coil sensitivities + Fourier transform + learnable sampling mask; unrolled stages alternate a CNN denoiser with a closed-form conjugate-gradient data-consistency update and an ADMM "personalized anomaly-aware" module. NetAD is a **fine-tuned CLIP** anomaly detector (pixel- and image-level adapters trained from 12 annotated cases per dataset) whose attention maps steer both k-space trajectory generation and reconstruction. Downstream evaluation: the **VLM-based (fine-tuned CLIP) detector** scores reconstructed fastMRI brain (T1w, FLAIR) and knee (PD) images with fastMRI+ labels at 4x/8x; PASS reports the highest AUC across anatomies, and "Mass" lesion classification accuracy 0.833 at 4x, above the fully-sampled reference (0.761). **No uncertainty quantification, no posterior sampling; deterministic.** — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.06849). Threat: F1 **medium** (physics recon -> VLM-family detector, but single-point; the "beats fully sampled" number is itself a sign that the VLM prior is injecting information, which is F1's prior-determined regime); F2 low; F3 none.
- **OmniMRI: A Unified Vision-Language Foundation Model for Generalist MRI Interpretation** — Xingxin He, Aurora Rofena, Ruimin Feng, Haozhe Liao, Zhaoye Zhou, Albert Jang, Fang Liu — arXiv 2508.17524 (Aug 24, 2025; **no venue on arXiv**; AuntMinnie reports an ISMRM 2026 presentation, unverified) — trained on 60 public datasets / 220k volumes / 19M slices; instructions include "Reconstruct the image from this under-sampled MRI data" and report generation; qualitative results at 2x/4x/6x on brain, breast, prostate, knee. The paper "does not specify whether reconstruction operates in k-space (with data consistency constraints) or image-domain", report generation is "presented as a standalone capability rather than ... a post-reconstruction step", and there is "no discussion of uncertainty quantification". — [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.17524); [GitHub](https://github.com/I3Tlab/OmniMRI). Threat: F3 **medium** (a unified medical VLM that "reconstructs" from undersampled data with no measurement constraint is precisely the object F3 would add data consistency to); F1 low-medium (it could be the MLLM in an F1 study); F2 none.
- **Vision-Language Controlled Deep Unfolding for Joint Medical Image Restoration and Segmentation** — Ping Chen et al. — arXiv 2601.23103 (Jan 2026; comment "18 pages, medical image"; no venue) — joint unfolding of restoration and segmentation with a frequency-aware Mamba block and VLM semantic priors; +0.92 dB PSNR, +9.76% Dice. Downstream is segmentation, not a VLM. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.23103). Threat low.
- **CARE: Training-Free Controllable Restoration for Medical Images via Dual-Latent Steering** — arXiv 2603.25026 (no venue) — one latent branch "enforces data fidelity and anatomical consistency", the other a generative prior; motivated by "overly aggressive restoration may introduce hallucinated details or alter diagnostically important structures"; no VLM downstream — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25026). Threat low.
- **Pixelwise Uncertainty Quantification of Accelerated MRI Reconstruction** — arXiv 2601.13236 — conformal quantile regression on E2E-VarNet reconstructions (fastMRI, R=2..10) to flag unreliable regions "without access to any ground-truth reference image"; motivation: "no mechanism exists to automatically assess the diagnostic quality of undersampled reconstructions". No VLM. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13236). Threat low (pixel-level, not task-level).
- Accepted physics-based reconstruction work with downstream evaluation (Mind the Detail, DynamicDPS, Image-less Active Sampling, seg-guided diverse reconstruction) feeds detectors, FastSurfer, a classifier or a segmenter (see KQ1(b)); none feeds a VQA model or report generator.
- A structured search of MICCAI 2025 titles found no reconstruction + report-generation/LLM paper — [MICCAI 2025 list](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2025/); searches over IEEE TMI / MedIA / Nature-family for "undersampled" + "report generation"/"VQA" returned only report-generation works without a reconstruction stage (e.g. ReMIND, mpLLM 2509.25889, BrainGPT Nature Comms 2025) — [Nature Comms](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-57426-0).

### Inferences
- The medical field has all the ingredients in separate accepted papers (DPS-style solvers with known operators; diverse data-consistent reconstructions feeding detectors; semantic-entropy hallucination detection for medical VQA at MICCAI 2025) but nobody has composed them. The composition "posterior samples -> medical VLM answer distribution" is unclaimed at any venue as of Sep 2026.
- PASS is the paper most likely to be extended into that composition (same group could add sampling); OmniMRI is the natural target for an F3 study.

### Gaps
- OmniMRI's reconstruction mechanism (k-space vs image domain) is not stated in the paper; I could not resolve it from the html.
- PASS's exact evaluation protocol for the VLM-based detector (whether it is the same NetAD used as prior, which would be circular) was not resolved from the html summary.
- I did not find, and cannot rule out, a 2026 TMI/MedIA paper coupling DPS to a report generator; my searches were keyword-based and TMI/MedIA tables of contents were not scanned.

## KQ3: Does any work assess whether a finding/answer is recoverable from undersampled/degraded measurements (hallucinated lesions affecting diagnosis; task-level "Looks Too Good To Be True"-style analysis)?

### Takeaway
Task-level recoverability is assessed in accepted work only with non-VLM downstream models (Mind the Detail, MICCAI 2025: rare pathologies missed even with diffusion resampling; Image-less Active Sampling, MICCAI 2024: diagnosis determinable from 7% of k-space) and, on the VLM side, only through refusal training (KIE-HVQA, NeurIPS 2025) or corruption benchmarks (DriveBench, ICCV 2025). No paper computes recoverability of a VLM answer from the measurement via the posterior; the "Looks Too Good To Be True" citation list contains no VLM pipeline.

### Cited Findings
- Mind the Detail: "existing techniques, even when considering resampling for diffusion-based reconstruction, can fail to reconstruct small and rare pathologies, thus leading to potentially wrong diagnosis decisions (false negatives)"; SDR raises detector recall at similar/better mAP — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00670).
- Image-less Active Sampling: disease inferred directly from undersampled k-space with performance "comparable with ML-based diagnosis, using fully sampled k-space data"; AUC 0.780 at 7% of k-space (per MICCAI page summary) — [MICCAI 2024](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2024/771-Paper2052.html).
- KIE-HVQA: model "capacity, under degraded input, to distinguish reliable visual information and answer accordingly", with refusal/occlusion rewards — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20168).
- DriveBench: VLMs answer "from general knowledge or textual cues rather than true visual grounding, especially under degraded or missing visual inputs" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.04003).
- ObsDriveBench (arXiv 2607.23537, Jul 2026, no venue): benchmark with an explicit "observability awareness" dimension (whether scenes remain understandable under fog/rain/snow/low light) over 14k train / 13k test questions with camera+LiDAR+radar, plus an ObsDrive model trained with SFT + adversarial RL; no restoration, no uncertainty metric stated — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23537).
- Clinical reader studies: a 2025 prostate DL-reconstruction study documented hallucinatory false positives and obscured true lesions, while a 7-reader knee study reported no hallucinated or omitted findings — [prostate, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11519109/); [knee, Radiology](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/radiol.242952). Neither involves VLMs or posterior analysis.
- "Looks Too Good To Be True" (2405.16475) citing papers (25 returned by S2 on 2026-09-23): SR/restoration hallucination metrics (Hallucination Score 2507.14367, CHEM 2512.09806, HalluGen-adjacent), RPU hallucination-aware DPS (2606.02331), MACS (2510.02208), Trustworthy SR (2505.12375), MARVEL (IEEE TMI 2026, cardiac CT motion), MLMI@MICCAI structural-uncertainty paper, a "Review of Hallucination Understanding in Large Language and Vision Models" (2510.00034); **none** feeds a VLM with posterior samples — [S2 API](https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/arXiv:2405.16475/citations?fields=title,venue,year,externalIds&limit=500).
- HalluGen (arXiv 2512.03345, no venue): 4,350 annotated hallucination images from 1,450 brain MR images for low-field enhancement; SHAFE metric; reference-free hallucination detectors — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03345).

### Inferences
- The recoverability question is being asked in three disconnected vocabularies: "false negatives from under-diverse posteriors" (MICCAI), "refusal under visual uncertainty" (NeurIPS OCR), and "observability awareness" (driving). None uses the posterior p(x|y) to decide whether an answer is measurement-determined; this leaves F1's three-way split unclaimed.
- The driving benchmarks' text-only controls (DriveBench) are the nearest accepted operationalization of "prior-determined answer"; F1 would replace that binary control with a posterior-sample entropy.

### Gaps
- I did not find any paper measuring per-lesion recoverability as a function of acceleration factor with a VLM/VQA model as the reader.
- ObsDriveBench's exact observability metric was not extractable from the abstract.

## KQ4 (OCR/scene text): Does anyone quantify character-level identifiability from degraded measurements via posterior sampling?

### Takeaway
No. Scene-text SR papers (accepted or arXiv) improve text fidelity with recognizer/OCR guidance and report OCR accuracy; the "uncertainty" in PRISM is an architectural residual encoder, not a posterior over characters; KIE-HVQA quantifies field-level refusal, not character-level identifiability; and no work draws multiple measurement-consistent samples and reads the character distribution off them.

### Cited Findings
- TAIR/TeReDiff (ICLR 2026 per project page): text spotting inside the denoising loop; evaluation is text-spotting accuracy; identifies "text-image hallucination" but does not quantify identifiability — [project page](https://cvlab-kaist.github.io/TAIR/).
- PRISM (arXiv 2605.13027, no venue): "slight errors in stroke topology may alter character identity"; "the text condition extracted from low-quality inputs can itself be unreliable, and a plausible global prior does not fully determine fine-grained stroke boundaries"; addressed by flow-matching prior rectification and a "Structure-guided Uncertainty-aware Residual Encoder" (single-step diffusion; no posterior sampling) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13027).
- Beyond OCR Accuracy: Text-Centric VQA Under Image Degradation (arXiv 2609.13815, Sep 12, 2026, no venue): 4,013 degraded images / 7,000 QA pairs (motion blur, low resolution, compression); modular pipelines reach 57.50% exact match vs 38.00% for the end-to-end VLM baseline; CER/WER are "unreliable predictors of downstream VQA performance" because semantic reasoning compensates for recognition errors — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13815). No posterior sampling.
- Restore Text First, Enhance Image Later (2510.21590) and TextSR (2505.23119): OCR-guided diffusion SR; no venue found; OCR metrics only — [2510.21590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21590), [2505.23119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23119).
- LPLC/LPLCv2 (license plates): legibility is an annotated label per plate with capture conditions, i.e. identifiability as supervised classification, not posterior analysis — [LPLCv2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08741).
- A search for "scene text recognition posterior samples / multiple hypotheses / character uncertainty" returned only diffusion-based recognizers (MDiff4STR 2512.01422, DiffusionSTR) and data synthesis (TextSSR), none quantifying identifiability from degraded measurements.

### Inferences
- The OCR vertical treats degradation as a fidelity problem (make OCR right) and, in KIE-HVQA, as a refusal problem; the posterior-entropy view of "which characters does y actually pin down" is absent, so F1 applied to text has no direct precedent. The nearest F2-like primitive is OCR-hypothesis-conditioned diffusion SR (TAIR, GLYPH-SR, TextSR), which prompts a restorer with a recognized string but never scores competing strings by measurement likelihood.

### Gaps
- ICDAR 2024/2025 proceedings were not scanned directly (searches only); a degraded-DocVQA-with-restoration paper there cannot be excluded.

### Inferences
