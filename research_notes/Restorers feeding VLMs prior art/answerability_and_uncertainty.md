# Answerability, identifiability and uncertainty of VLM/MLLM answers under image degradation

Scope: 2023 – Sep 2026 (VizWiz-era foundations as context). Venue filter: CVPR/ICCV/ECCV/WACV/ICML/ICLR/NeurIPS/AAAI/IJCAI/ACL/EMNLP/NAACL/COLM/TPAMI/IJCV/TIP/Nature. "Verified via" states exactly how the venue was confirmed (arXiv comment line, proceedings URL, OpenReview API `venueid`, ACL Anthology page, AAAI OJS page). Anything not confirmed from a proceedings page or the arXiv comment line is labelled UNVERIFIED. Semantic Scholar's public API rate-limited (HTTP 429) most citation queries; one successful "cited-by" pull is reported where obtained.

Legend for per-paper fields: **FM** = measurement/forward model used (Y/N); **GS** = generative samples used (Y/N); **MC** = measurement-consistent samples (Y/N/n.a.); **Threat** = F1 / F2 / F3 with one-line reason. Field short-hands: F1 = answer identifiability via MLLM answer entropy over measurement-consistent diffusion posterior samples x ~ p(x|y), split into measurement-determined / prior-determined / undetermined; F2 = hypothesis-conditioned evidence p(y|q,a) via text-conditioned inverse solver + Bayes factor; F3 = measurement-consistent self-recovery inside unified MLLMs.

## KQ1. Who has formally defined answerability/unanswerability under degradation, and how (human labels, MLLM self-judgment, IQA scores, occluded-character annotation, or a measurement/physics model)?

### Takeaway
Every accepted paper found defines (un)answerability operationally, via (a) human/crowd labels (VizWiz lineage, UNK-VQA, MoHoBench, AbstainEQA, KoNA), (b) construction rules that guarantee unanswerability (object-removal inpainting in CertainlyUncertain; option/image mismatch in MM-UPD; synthetic degradation with pixel-level "unreadable" masks in KIE-HVQA; procedurally removed evidence in TRAPSBench), or (c) MLLM/LLM filtering plus human verification. No accepted paper defines answerability through a measurement/forward model of the degradation or through the posterior p(x|y); the closest are KIE-HVQA (synthetic degradation masks + MLLM validation that characters are unreadable) and, outside VQA, restoration-theory papers that bound hallucination from the forward operator alone.

### Inferences
- The accepted literature defines answerability by *labels or construction*; the only accepted work that ties unanswerability to the degradation process is KIE-HVQA, and even there the label is computed offline from known character masks, then trained into the model. Nobody infers "is the answer determined by y" from y itself with a forward model.
- The CertainlyUncertain epistemic/aleatoric split and TRAPSBench's "undeterminable from visual evidence" are the two accepted framings a CVPR reviewer is most likely to raise against F1's measurement-/prior-/undetermined split; both lack a posterior and a measurement model, which is exactly the gap F1 fills.
- KIE-HVQA is a natural evaluation testbed for F1 (character-level ground truth of what is unreadable), and MoHoBench/VizWiz-LF are natural motivation citations.

## KQ2. Does any work use generative samples (restorations, inpaintings, completions) to estimate VLM answer uncertainty or answerability? With or without measurement consistency?

### Takeaway
No accepted paper measures VLM answer entropy over posterior samples of a degraded image. The closest precedents split into (i) VLMs applied to *many* diffusion SR samples for *selection*, not uncertainty (Korkmaz et al., TCSVT 2025 — journal outside the filter); (ii) semantic-entropy-style methods that perturb the image with noise/blur/crops rather than posterior samples (VSE, ECCV 2026; VL-Uncertainty and Uncertainty-o, arXiv-only; HEDGE, arXiv-only); (iii) generative editing to *construct* unanswerable data (CertainlyUncertain, ICLR 2025; one inpainting as an aid in CAPTURe, ICCV 2025); and (iv) restoration-side theory on the inherent uncertainty of the posterior (NeurIPS 2024, ICLR 2024) with no VQA. None uses measurement-consistent posterior samples to define answerability.

### Cited Findings

**Sampling-based VLM uncertainty (perturbation, not posterior)**
- **Visual Semantic Entropy (VSE)** (Huy et al.; ECCV 2026 per arXiv comment "Accepted at ECCV2026"; arXiv 2606.31407; not yet on Springer/CVF, so accepted-but-not-in-proceedings). Argues Semantic Entropy underestimates uncertainty because "overconfident visual embeddings suppress output diversity under stochastic decoding", and that text/joint perturbation methods (VL-Uncertainty) measure prompt sensitivity rather than visual ambiguity; VSE perturbs only the image with fixed small Gaussian noise (sigma = 20; also tests affine, color, Gaussian blur), clusters answers with DeBERTa similarity and scores frequency-weighted pairwise prototype distance; AUC gains up to 9.2% on AOKVQA/OKVQA/MMVet/VILP/VLM-are-biased; degraded/low-quality inputs not studied — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31407); [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.31407). FM N; GS N (analytic noise perturbations, not generative); MC N. Threat: F1 medium-high (the closest accepted "image-side answer entropy" method; a reviewer will ask why posterior samples beat local Gaussian perturbations; the answer is that VSE's perturbations are not measurement-consistent and do not represent p(x|y)); F2 none; F3 none.
- **Dropout Decoding** (Fang, Yang, Chen, Zhao, Zhou; NeurIPS 2025; arXiv 2412.06474). Verified via arXiv comment "Accepted to ... NeurIPS 2025". Projects each visual token to text space and decomposes its uncertainty into aleatoric and epistemic parts; masks uncertain visual tokens at decoding — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06474). FM N; GS N. Threat: F1 low (token-level epistemic uncertainty inside the MLLM, not answer-level identifiability); F3 low.
- **Thinking in Uncertainty / LEAD** (Xu et al.; CVPR 2026; arXiv 2603.13366). Verified via [CVF Open Access CVPR 2026](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Xu_Thinking_in_Uncertainty_Mitigating_Hallucinations_in_MLRMs_with_Latent_Entropy-Aware_CVPR_2026_paper.html) (pages 11064–11075 per search snippet). High-entropy transition tokens ("because", "however", "wait") correlate with hallucination; LEAD replaces the one-hot token with a probability-weighted embedding mixture and injects a visual guidance vector at high-entropy steps — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13366). FM N; GS N; no degradation. Threat: F1 none-low (decoding-time entropy, not answerability); F3 low.
- **When to Think and When to Look** (Bi et al.; CVPR 2026; arXiv 2511.15613). Verified via arXiv comment "Accepted to CVPR 2026". Uncertainty-guided "lookback" to the image during reasoning on MMMU — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15613). Threat: none for F1/F2/F3.
- **The Cost of Reasoning** (Welch, Konuk, Smith; ECCV 2026 per Springer chapter listing "Computer Vision – ECCV 2026", DOI 10.1007/978-3-032-37196-6_27; arXiv 2603.16728). Verified via [Springer chapter page](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-37196-6_27) (title/series from search snippet; page itself blocked by bot check — partially verified). Chain-of-thought degrades token-probability UQ via "implicit answer conditioning" while "agreement-based consistency remains robust and often improves under reasoning" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16728). Threat: F1 low (supports using sample-agreement rather than token probabilities as the uncertainty readout, which favours F1's entropy-over-samples design).
- **VAUQ** (Park, Oh, Choi, Du, Li; ACL 2026 Findings per arXiv comment; arXiv 2602.21054). Image-Information Score measures how strongly the output depends on visual evidence — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21054). FM N; GS N. Threat: F1 low-medium (an "is the answer image-grounded or prior-driven" score is conceptually adjacent to F1's measurement- vs prior-determined split, but computed by ablating the image, not by sampling the posterior).
- **Revisiting Confidence Calibration for Misclassification Detection in VLMs** (Huang et al.; ICLR 2026). Verified via [ML Anthology](https://mlanthology.org/iclr/2026/huang2026iclr-revisiting-a/) and [OpenReview PDF](https://openreview.net/pdf/e979c6c5de001aa2d33bc875b3ab9a603d6a39a2.pdf). Standard calibration impairs misclassification detection; post-hoc meta-network temperature. Threat: none (CLIP-style classification, no degradation).
- **Post-hoc Probabilistic VLMs** (ICLR 2026 per arXiv comment "Published at ICLR 2026"; arXiv 2412.06014): Bayesian posterior over CLIP last layers — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06014). Threat: none.

**Generative samples used with VLMs (selection / construction, not uncertainty)**
- **Leveraging VLMs to Select Trustworthy SR Samples** (Korkmaz, Tekalp, Dogan; IEEE TCSVT 2025 per arXiv comment and journal-ref — journal is outside the venue filter, but this is the single closest method to F1, so listed here). Generates 100 diverse SR samples per natural image (324 for MNIST digits) from a latent diffusion model, prompts BLIP-2/GPT-4o ("What is the digit?", artifact/quality queries) to rank samples, ensembles the top-5; reports GPT-4o 95.2% consistency across 20 paraphrased prompts; Trustworthiness Score = 0.2 CLIP similarity + 0.3 edge-SSIM + 0.5 wavelet artifact penalty; "no explicit enforcement of consistency with low-resolution input beyond the diffusion model's inherent conditioning"; 65-participant human study on MNIST/Urban100/DIV2K/BSD100/Set14 — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20832); [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.20832). Predecessor "Trustworthy SR: Resolving Ambiguity ... via Diffusion Models and Human Feedback" (ICIP 2024, arXiv 2402.07597) had humans select samples — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07597). FM N (conditioning only); GS Y; MC N. Threat: F1 HIGH in spirit / medium in substance: it already asks a VLM "what is the digit?" over hundreds of diffusion SR samples of an ambiguous LR image, but uses answers to *pick* a restoration rather than to measure answer entropy or define identifiability, and imposes no measurement consistency. The F1 paper must cite it and position identifiability (entropy over p(x|y)) as the inverse of their selection problem; F2 low; F3 low.
- **CertainlyUncertain** (ICLR 2025) — see KQ1: LaMa inpainting used to manufacture unanswerable pairs, not to estimate uncertainty — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.01942).
- **CAPTURe** (ICCV 2025) — see KQ1: one FLUX.1-Fill inpainting used as auxiliary input — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15485).
- **Text-guided Explorable Image Super-resolution** (Gandikota, Chandramouli; CVPR 2024; arXiv 2403.01124). Verified via arXiv comment "CVPR 2024". Zero-shot text-guided exploration of SR solutions that "preserve data consistency with the low-resolution inputs" by modifying T2I diffusion sampling or adding language guidance to zero-shot diffusion restoration — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01124). FM Y; GS Y; MC Y. No VQA, no uncertainty. Threat: F1 low; F2 MEDIUM-HIGH (this is exactly the machinery F2 needs: text-hypothesis-conditioned, measurement-consistent reconstructions; it stops at exploration and never computes p(y|q,a) or a Bayes factor, but a reviewer will see F2 as "explorable SR + a likelihood readout"); F3 low.
- **Your Diffusion Model is Secretly a Zero-Shot Classifier** (Li et al.; ICCV 2023; arXiv 2303.16203). Verified via arXiv comment "In ICCV 2023". Uses text-conditional diffusion density estimates p(x | text) and Bayes' rule to classify clean images — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16203). FM N; GS N (likelihood evaluation). Threat: F2 MEDIUM (establishes "diffusion likelihood under each text hypothesis + Bayes" for clean x; F2's novelty must be the measurement y and the inverse solver, i.e., p(y|q,a) = ∫ p(y|x) p(x|q,a) dx rather than p(x|a)); F1 none; F3 none.

**Restoration-side theory on posterior uncertainty (no VQA)**
- **Looks Too Good To Be True** (Cohen, Kligvasser, Rivlin, Freedman; NeurIPS 2024; arXiv 2405.16475). Verified via [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/2847d43f17410c5beb25b2736c3ae778-Abstract-Conference.html) and [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=85tu7K06i3). Defines the inherent uncertainty of a restoration problem information-theoretically and proves that "the global minimal uncertainty in generative models grows in tandem with perception" and that perfect perceptual quality "entails at least twice the inherent uncertainty of the restoration problem"; experiments on SR and inpainting — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16475). FM Y; GS Y (evaluation); MC n.a. Cited-by pulled successfully (25 records): includes "Hallucination-Aware Diffusion Sampling for Inverse Problems via Robust Prior Updates" (arXiv 2606.02331), "MACS: Measurement-Aware Consistency Sampling" (2510.02208), "Hallucination Score ... Generative Image SR" (2507.14367), "Trustworthy Image SR via Generative Pseudoinverse" (2505.12375), "CHEM" (2512.09806), "Evaluating structural uncertainty in accelerated MRI" (2503.10527); none is a VQA/VLM answerability paper — [S2 API](https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/arXiv:2405.16475/citations?fields=title,venue,year,externalIds&limit=500). Threat: F1 medium (gives the pixel-level H(X|Y) that F1's question-level entropy H(A|Y) coarsens; the F1 paper should explicitly relate the two; no VQA); F2 low; F3 low.
- **From Posterior Sampling to Meaningful Diversity in Image Restoration** (Cohen, Manor, Bahat, Michaeli; ICLR 2024; arXiv 2310.16047). Verified via arXiv comment "Accepted for ICLR 2024". Argues i.i.d. posterior samples are dominated by the mode (inpainted sky = clouds) and the posterior is "heavy-tailed along semantically interesting directions", proposing small meaningfully diverse solution sets instead — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16047). FM Y; GS Y; MC Y. Threat: F1 medium (a direct methodological caveat: plain posterior sampling can miss the rare semantic alternatives that flip an answer, so F1's entropy estimate may be biased toward "measurement-determined"; F1 should address heavy tails, e.g., with meaningful-diversity sampling or importance weighting); F2 low; F3 none.
- **Semantic uncertainty intervals for disentangled latent spaces** (Sankaranarayanan et al.; NeurIPS 2022 — outside window, context only). Calibrated intervals on semantic latents (hair colour, object location) propagated through a generator — [NeurIPS 2022](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/290141d6bfd7ea4d3f4483d126609bf6-Abstract-Conference.html). Threat: F1 low-medium (earliest "uncertainty over semantic attributes of reconstructions" precedent; no VQA/MLLM).

### Inferences
- The specific combination in F1 — measurement-consistent posterior samples x ~ p(x|y), an MLLM answering q on each, and the entropy/agreement of those answers as an identifiability score — has no accepted precedent. The three pieces exist separately: posterior samplers with theory (NeurIPS 2024, ICLR 2024), VLMs querying many SR samples (TCSVT 2025), and answer entropy over image perturbations (ECCV 2026 VSE).
- The reviewer-facing risk for F1 is not a scoop but a "why not just VSE / Uncertainty-o with a restorer in the loop?" question; the answer is measurement consistency (perturbations of y are not samples of p(x|y)) and the three-way split, which perturbation methods cannot produce because they have no prior-only counterpart.
- Heavy-tailed posteriors (ICLR 2024) are the main technical objection to F1's entropy estimator.

### Gaps
- No accepted or arXiv paper was found that uses *multiple inpaintings/amodal completions* to compute VQA answer disagreement; amodal-completion work (e.g., "Integrating MLLM Knowledge into Amodal Completion", arXiv 2603.28333, venue unverified) uses MLLMs to guide completion, not to score answer uncertainty — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28333).
- Springer page for "The Cost of Reasoning" could not be opened (bot challenge); ECCV 2026 attribution rests on the Springer listing title in search results.

## KQ3. Which works find internal signals (token probabilities, hidden states) track answerability under degradation, and how would those compare to measurement-grounded approaches?

### Takeaway
Accepted work shows that VLMs *encode* abstention-relevant information internally but fail to verbalize it (TRAPSBench, COLM 2026; EMNLP 2025 visually-absent-token neurons), that verbalized confidence degrades with corruption severity (TrustNLP@NAACL 2025 workshop), and that consistency-based signals beat token probabilities under reasoning (ECCV 2026). The strongest degradation-specific internal-signal results are arXiv-only (Ferdous 2607.22034/2607.24440: token-probability AUROC 0.92–0.99 vs verbalized ~0.50, both collapsing under severe under-exposure; Reading Between the Lines 2511.19806: intermediate-layer probes for OCR abstention). All are model-internal and lack any notion of whether the *measurement* determines the answer.

### Cited Findings
- **TRAPSBench** (COLM 2026): "VLMs can internally distinguish when abstention is required, but fail to express it"; spontaneous restraint poor across 16 VLMs of five families — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13167). Internal signal: hidden-state probes on synthetic physics videos with removed evidence.
- **Unveiling ... Visually Absent Tokens** (EMNLP 2025 Main): VA neurons in FFN layers signal missing visual support; used for detection and output refinement — [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1092/).
- **Do LVLMs Know What They Know?** (EMNLP 2025 Findings): probabilistic confidence is the best of three signals but perception of knowledge boundaries has "substantial room for improvement" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19111).
- **The Cost of Reasoning** (ECCV 2026): token-probability UQ degrades under CoT because of implicit answer conditioning; agreement-based consistency stays robust — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16728).
- **Know What You do Not Know** (Borszukovszki, de Jong, Valdenegro-Toro; TrustNLP Workshop @ NAACL 2025 — workshop, not main conference; arXiv 2504.03440). Verified via arXiv comment and [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2025.trustnlp-main.16/). Three VLMs on corrupted images: "the severity of the corruption negatively impacted the models' ability to estimate their uncertainty and the models also showed overconfidence in most of the experiments" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.03440). FM N (ImageNet-C-style corruptions); GS N. Threat: F1 low-medium (establishes that verbalized uncertainty fails under corruption, motivating an external, measurement-grounded signal; no answerability definition).
- **Small VLMs Know When They Are Wrong But Cannot Say So** (Ferdous; arXiv 2607.22034, 24 Jul 2026; comment "15 pages, 4 figures", no venue — arXiv-only). Qwen2-VL-2B and SmolVLM across six photographic degradations x three severities, 3,800 predictions: verbalized confidence nearly constant (0.87–0.90) with AUROC ~0.50, internal mean token probability AUROC 0.92–0.99; under severe under-exposure accuracy collapses (0.99 -> 0.22; 0.97 -> 0.42) "while both confidence signals barely move, and internal error-detection falls to chance" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22034). FM N; GS N.
- **Bigger or Cheaper?** (Ferdous; arXiv 2607.24440, 27 Jul 2026; no venue — arXiv-only). 5,700 predictions, Qwen2-VL family: internal AUROC rises 0.80 -> 0.98 from 2B to 7B while verbalized stays 0.61–0.69; 4-bit quantization costs -1.6 accuracy points but internal AUROC drops 0.95 -> 0.80 and verbalized parse rate 99% -> 64% — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24440). FM N; GS N.
- **Reading Between the Lines** (Yao et al.; arXiv 2511.19806; no venue — arXiv-only). Latent Representation Probing for STVQA abstention: attention over image tokens across layers and majority-vote ensembles of single-layer hidden-state probes; "optimal signals emerge from intermediate rather than final layers"; motivated by "Can VLMs know when they can't see?" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19806). FM N; GS N.
- **Grounded or Guessing? (BICR)** (Khanmohammadi et al.; arXiv 2605.10893; no venue). Probe on hidden states with a ranking loss against a blacked-out-image pass, to detect answers "driven entirely by language priors" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10893). FM N; GS N. Conceptually adjacent to F1's "prior-determined" class, but detected by image ablation.
- **Same Answer, Different Representations** (arXiv 2602.06652; no venue): answers stay fixed while internal representations drift under perturbations such as text overlays — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06652).
- **When Thinking Hurts** (Singal; EIML@ICML 2026 workshop oral; arXiv 2607.08059): answer-entropy AUROC collapses to 0.492 in Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking on POPE; chain entropy is a better signal — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08059).

### Inferences
- Internal-signal methods estimate *whether the model will be right*, conditional on its own perception; F1 estimates *whether any perceiver could be right* given y. Under severe degradation the arXiv results show internal signals themselves collapse (AUROC to chance under under-exposure), which is precisely the regime where a measurement-grounded posterior remains well defined. This is a clean positioning argument, and the numbers above can be quoted (with the arXiv-only caveat).
- BICR and VAUQ's "image-grounded vs prior-driven" scores are the internal-signal analogue of F1's measurement- vs prior-determined split; F1 should show cases where the image is *used* yet the answer is still prior-determined (posterior samples disagree), which ablation-based scores cannot separate.

### Gaps
- No accepted top-venue paper was found that evaluates token-probability or hidden-state answerability signals specifically as a function of degradation severity; the degradation-severity studies are arXiv-only or workshop.

## KQ4. Information-theoretic treatments of "how much question-relevant information survives degradation" for VQA/captioning (task-relevant information loss, semantic rate-distortion for VQA)?

### Takeaway
Accepted information-theoretic work exists on either side of F1 but not at the intersection: restoration theory bounds pixel-level uncertainty/hallucination from the forward model (NeurIPS 2024; arXiv 2605.13146), and VLM work quantifies information lost inside the model's connector (EMNLP 2025 Findings) or shows VQA accuracy overstates preserved information because linguistic priors fill gaps (arXiv 2608.01848). No accepted paper defines a question-conditional quantity such as I(A; Y | q) or a semantic rate-distortion curve for VQA under physical degradation.

### Cited Findings
- **Looks Too Good To Be True** (NeurIPS 2024): defines inherent uncertainty of the restoration problem and proves the uncertainty–perception tradeoff; relates distortion, uncertainty and perception — [NeurIPS](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/2847d43f17410c5beb25b2736c3ae778-Abstract-Conference.html).
- **Lost in Embeddings** (Li et al.; EMNLP 2025 Findings). Verified via [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1235/). Connector projection distorts local geometry (k-NN divergence 40–60%); patch-level reconstruction of visual embeddings localizes information loss, and "areas of high information loss reliably predict instances where models struggle" on visually grounded QA — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11986). FM N. Threat: F1 low-medium (an "information loss predicts failure" story, but loss is internal to the model, not in the measurement; F1 can contrast measurement-side loss vs model-side loss).
- **Demystifying the Visual Quality Paradox in MLLMs** (Xing et al.; arXiv 2506.15645; OpenReview/dblp list only CoRR 2025 — arXiv-only). Controlled degradations across MLLMs/benchmarks; "model, task, and even individual-instance performance can improve when images deviate from human-perceived fidelity"; proposes VQ-TTT — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15645). Threat: F1 low-medium (shows perceptual quality is not task information; supports F1's premise that answerability must be defined per question, not by IQA).
- **Less Detail, Better Answers** (Han et al.; CVPR Workshops 2026 per arXiv comment "Accepted to CVPRW 2026" — workshop; arXiv 2604.04838). Deliberate degradation improves VQA on some tasks — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04838). Threat: F3 low.
- **Decoupling semantics from vision** (Gao et al.; arXiv 2608.01848; no venue). Shows downstream-task accuracy overestimates text preservation because "a few recognizable keywords can be sufficient to infer the correct answer despite substantial unreadable content"; introduces ZeroSense benchmark with low semantic correlation — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01848). Threat: F1 low-medium (empirical evidence for the "prior-determined" class: answers recovered from priors, not from the measurement).
- **On Hallucinations in Inverse Problems: Fundamental Limits and Provable Assessment Methods** (Iagaru, Gottschling, Hansen, Garnier; arXiv 2605.13146, May 2026; no venue). Derives "necessary and sufficient conditions for hallucinations, together with computable bounds on their magnitude that depend only on the forward model" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13146). FM Y. Threat: F1 medium (a forward-model-only identifiability theory at pixel level; F1 lifts it to the answer level); F2 low.
- **Separating Intrinsic Ambiguity from Estimation Uncertainty in Deep Generative Models for Linear Inverse Problems** (Guo, Deng, Grover; arXiv 2605.15050, May 2026; no venue). Decomposes posterior uncertainty into "ambiguity inherent to the forward operator" vs inference uncertainty via a cascade formulation with simulation-based calibration — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15050). FM Y; GS Y; MC Y. Threat: F1 medium (its intrinsic-ambiguity vs estimation-uncertainty split is the pixel-level analogue of F1's measurement-determined vs undetermined split; no VQA/MLLM).
- **Rate-Distortion Limits for Multimodal Retrieval** (ICCV Workshops 2025 — workshop). [CVF ICCVW](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025W/MRR%202025/papers/Chen_Rate-Distortion_Limits_for_Multimodal_Retrieval_Theory_Optimal_Codes_and_Finite-Sample_ICCVW_2025_paper.pdf). Retrieval, not VQA. Threat: none.
- **Identification and Honest Recovery from Semantic Observation Kernels** (Dixon; arXiv 2607.23130; no venue). Frames recovering a posterior over "meaningful states" from LLM phrase probabilities as a semiparametric inverse problem with identification guarantees — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23130). Threat: F1 low (text-only; the word "identification" overlaps with F1's vocabulary, worth a citation to pre-empt confusion).

### Inferences
- The term "identifiability" is unclaimed in the VQA-under-degradation literature; the nearest uses are pixel-level (arXiv 2605.13146, 2605.15050) and text-only (2607.23130). F1 can define answer identifiability as a property of (q, y, forward model) and cite NeurIPS 2024 for the pixel-level counterpart.
- A semantic rate–distortion or I(A;Y|q) curve versus degradation severity would be new; the closest empirical data are the "quality paradox" (arXiv) and "keywords suffice" (arXiv) observations.

### Gaps
- No accepted paper computes a question-conditional information measure under physical degradation; this is a genuine gap rather than a search failure (queries on "semantic rate distortion VQA", "task-relevant information degradation VQA", "identifiable answer degraded image" returned only compression/retrieval or model-internal work).
